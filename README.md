## 🧮 Mathematical Formulations & Flight Physics Deck

<div align="center">
<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <b>1. TCAS II Closest Point of Approach ($\tau_{CPA}$)</b><br/>
      $$\vec{r}_{rel} = \vec{r}_2 - \vec{r}_1, \quad \vec{v}_{rel} = \vec{v}_2 - \vec{v}_1$$
      $$\tau_{CPA} = -\frac{\vec{r}_{rel} \cdot \vec{v}_{rel}}{\|\vec{v}_{rel}\|^2}$$
      $$d_{CPA} = \|\vec{r}_{rel} + \vec{v}_{rel} \cdot \tau_{CPA}\|$$
      <i>Triggers Resolution Advisory (RA) when $\tau_{CPA} \le 25\text{s}$ & $d_{CPA} \le 1.0\text{ NM}$.</i>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <b>2. Orbital Hohmann Transfer Total $\Delta v$ Budget</b><br/>
      $$a_{trans} = \frac{r_1 + r_2}{2}$$
      $$\Delta v_1 = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r_1}}\left(\sqrt{\frac{2r_2}{r_1 + r_2}} - 1\right)$$
      $$\Delta v_2 = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r_2}}\left(1 - \sqrt{\frac{2r_1}{r_1 + r_2}}\right)$$
      $$\Delta v_{total} = \Delta v_1 + \Delta v_2, \quad t_{TOF} = \pi \sqrt{\frac{a_{trans}^3}{\mu}}$$
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <b>3. Hypersonic Stagnation Heat Flux (Fay-Riddell)</b><br/>
      $$\dot{q}_{ws} = 0.763 \, \text{Pr}^{-0.6} \left(\rho_w \mu_w\right)^{0.1} \left(\rho_s \mu_s\right)^{0.4} \left(h_0 - h_w\right) \sqrt{\left(\frac{du_e}{dx}\right)_s}$$
      <i>Governs thermal protection system (TPS) ablation rates during planetary entry.</i>
    </td>

      <b>4. Space Debris Collision Probability ($P_c$)</b><br/>
      $$P_c = \frac{1}{2\pi \sigma_x \sigma_y} \iint_{x^2 + y^2 \le R_A^2} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{(x - x_e)^2}{\sigma_x^2} + \frac{(y - y_e)^2}{\sigma_y^2}\right]\right) dx \, dy$$
      <i>Evaluates covariance ellipsoid intersection in the B-plane.</i>
